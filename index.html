Notchly Privacy Policy

Notchly – Live Island Alerts · Developer: Faqeed Hassan · Effective October 4, 2026

In short: Notchly reads your notifications and some phone states only to show them on the island around your front camera. Everything is processed on your phone. The app has no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It collects, stores and shares no personal data. The optional Notchly Pro purchase is handled by Google Play.

What Notchly is

Notchly is an Android app that draws a small, island-shaped overlay around your phone's front camera. Notifications, music, calls, timers, recordings, deliveries, downloads and system events (such as charging or Bluetooth) appear on it, and you can open them or expand them into a card with controls.

Data the app accesses, and why

To work, Notchly needs to read some information on your phone. This information is used only on your device, only while it is being shown, and it is never saved, uploaded or shared.

Notifications (Notification access)

  • The posting app's name and icon, and the notification's title, text, images, progress, timers and buttons.
  • For music: the song title, artist, album art and playback position, and the playback controls.
  • Used only to show the notification on the island, for the apps you choose in the app's settings, and to let you use its buttons (for example Reply, Pause or Decline) from the island.

Accessibility service

  • Lets the island sit above the status bar so you can tap it around the camera.
  • Reads the position of the status bar icons, so the island can make room for them.
  • Notes the package name of the app currently open, so a live activity (a delivery, ride or route) steps aside while you are in that app.
  • Notices which system windows are on screen (the lock screen, the control centre or a fullscreen app), so the island can react to unlocking, stay in place over the control centre and hide over fullscreen video.
  • It does not read the content of your screen or what you type, and it does not perform actions for you.

Phone and connection states

  • Battery level and charging, battery saver, ringer mode, Do Not Disturb, airplane mode, flashlight, Wi-Fi and hotspot on/off, and screen lock/unlock.
  • The name and battery level of Bluetooth audio devices (such as earbuds) when they connect.
  • Used only to show these events on the island, as you switch them on in the app's settings.

Installed apps list

  • The names and icons of apps that have a launcher icon, shown on the app selection screen so you can choose which apps may use the island.

Notchly Pro purchases

Notchly Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; Notchly never sees your payment details. To know whether Pro is unlocked, Notchly asks the Google Play app on your phone whether this Google account has bought it, and remembers the answer in its private storage. Notchly itself sends nothing anywhere. Purchases are covered by Google's privacy policy and Google Play's terms.

What is stored

Only your Notchly settings (such as the island's size and position and which apps are allowed) and whether Notchly Pro is unlocked are saved, in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not backed up to the cloud by the app. Uninstalling Notchly deletes them.

What is collected or shared

Nothing. Notchly does not request internet access, and it contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking. No data leaves your device, and no data is sold or shared with anyone.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: to draw the island.
  • Notification access and the accessibility service: as described above. The app explains each before you turn it on, and you can turn either off at any time in Android Settings.
  • Post notifications and foreground service: to keep the island running, with a notification that has a Stop button.
  • Run at startup: to bring the island back after the phone restarts, if you had it on.
  • Network state, Wi-Fi state and Bluetooth connect: to notice Wi-Fi, hotspot and Bluetooth device events. These permissions do not give internet access.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Notchly Pro and check whether it is unlocked, through the Google Play app on your phone.

Children

Notchly is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
